# HIPAA compliance kit

Synari is software. HIPAA compliance belongs to the organization that runs it (the covered entity
or business associate) and depends on how it is hosted, configured and operated. There is no
official "HIPAA certification"; organizations are assessed against the HIPAA Security, Privacy and
Breach Notification Rules, often through a third-party framework such as HITRUST CSF or a SOC 2
report with a HIPAA mapping.

This folder helps you run Synari in a way that stands up to that assessment:

| Document | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [controls-matrix.md](controls-matrix.md) | See each HIPAA requirement, what Synari does about it, what you must do, and how to produce evidence |
| [operator-checklist.md](operator-checklist.md) | Go live: the configuration and paperwork to finish before storing real patient data |
| [data-flows.md](data-flows.md) | Know where protected health information (PHI) goes, including every third-party service |
| [baa-checklist.md](baa-checklist.md) | Sign Business Associate Agreements with every vendor that touches PHI |
| [risk-analysis-template.md](risk-analysis-template.md) | Complete the risk analysis required by 45 CFR 164.308(a)(1) |
| [policies/](policies/) | Start your written policies and procedures from templates |

These documents are templates and guidance, not legal advice. Have them reviewed by your
privacy/security officer or counsel and adapt them to your organization and state law.
