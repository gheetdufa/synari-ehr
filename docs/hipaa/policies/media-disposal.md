# Retention, disposal and media re-use policy

**Owner:** [Security officer]

- **Retention:** client records are kept [7] years after the last date of service (longer for
  minors: until [age 25 / state rule]), or longer if state law requires (Settings → Security →
  Record retention). Audit logs are kept at least 6 years. Good Faith Estimates at least 6 years.
- **Disposal of records:** after retention ends, [security officer] reviews the list of archived
  clients past retention and approves disposal. Synari crypto-shreds files (destroys their keys);
  records of the disposal are kept.
- **Devices and media:** laptops and phones use full-disk encryption. Before re-use or disposal,
  drives are securely wiped or physically destroyed by [vendor with certificate]. Cloud volumes and
  backups are deleted through the provider; keys are destroyed.
- **Paper:** shredded with a cross-cut shredder or a certified service.
