# Access control policy

**Owner:** [Security officer] · **Reviewed:** [date] · **Applies to:** everyone with a Synari account.

1. **Unique accounts.** Every workforce member has their own Synari account. Accounts are never
   shared. Two-factor authentication is required for all staff.
2. **Least privilege.** Roles are assigned by job need:
   - Owner: [named individuals only].
   - Clinician: access level "basic" by default; "billing", "full client list" or "entire
     practice" only with [security officer] approval.
   - Practice manager, biller, scheduler: no clinical record access.
   Clients are placed on the care team only of clinicians who treat them.
3. **Granting and changing access.** [Practice manager] invites new staff after [HR step] and
   records the role and approver. Role changes are approved by [security officer].
4. **Reviews.** Every quarter, [security officer] reviews the team list, roles, supervisors and
   care-team assignments and removes anything no longer needed. The review is documented.
5. **Emergency ("break-glass") access.** Staff may use Emergency access only when a client's
   safety or care requires it and the client's clinicians aren't available. The reason must be
   specific. Owners review every use within [2 business days]; misuse is handled under the
   sanctions policy.
6. **Termination.** Accounts are deactivated in Synari (Settings → Team → Deactivate) on the last
   day of work or immediately on involuntary termination. Deactivation ends all sessions.
7. **Sessions.** Staff sessions end after [30] idle minutes. Staff lock their screens when away.
