# Policy templates

Starting points for the written policies HIPAA requires (45 CFR 164.316). Replace the bracketed
parts, adapt to your organization and state law, have them reviewed, and train staff on them.
They are templates, not legal advice.

- [access-control.md](access-control.md): who gets which role, reviews, break-glass, termination
- [audit-log-review.md](audit-log-review.md): what to review and how often
- [incident-response.md](incident-response.md): security incidents and breach notification
- [contingency-plan.md](contingency-plan.md): backups, disaster recovery, emergency mode
- [media-disposal.md](media-disposal.md): retention, disposal and device re-use
- [sanctions.md](sanctions.md): consequences for violations
- [workforce-training.md](workforce-training.md): security awareness training
