# Sanctions policy

Workforce members who violate security or privacy policies face sanctions proportionate to the
violation, from retraining and written warning to suspension of access, termination and
reporting to licensing boards or authorities where required. Examples: sharing passwords, viewing
records without a job need, misusing emergency access, sending PHI to personal email. Sanctions
are documented and kept for six years. Sanctions are applied consistently and never in
retaliation for reporting a problem in good faith.
