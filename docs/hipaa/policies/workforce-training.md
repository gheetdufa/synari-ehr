# Security awareness training

All workforce members complete training at hire (before getting a Synari account) and yearly,
covering: this organization's privacy and security policies; two-factor authentication and
phishing; minimum necessary access and the consequences of snooping; emergency access rules;
secure messaging instead of email or text for clinical content; recognizing and reporting
incidents; device security (screen locks, disk encryption, no PHI on personal devices); Good
Faith Estimates and the right-of-access process. [Security officer] keeps attendance records for
six years.
